

--(Binary) cross entropy

Cross entropy, being the standard metric for comparing probability distributions.
BCE = cross entropy simplified for two classes (with true and predicted label).

Can be used for image segmentation, eg. classifying foreground vs. background structures.

--Dice loss

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dice-S%C3%B8rensen_coefficient

Models overlap by intersection divided by union.
Comparison with BCE: this models overlap, while BCE measures pixel classification.

I figured this doesn't work very well on the task of identifying vertical and thin bar-like structures.
Does not promote estimation of vertical stripes, but instead of blurry patches.

--Combined Dice + BCE

Simply combines both, intending to compensate each other's weaknesses.

--Soft skeletonize

See: https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/Skeleton-Recall
Supposed to perform better on "thin" structures.
Unfortunately didn't work as expected / hoped for.

--Focal loss

Read: https://medium.com/visionwizard/understanding-focal-loss-a-quick-read-b914422913e7
Variant of cross-entropy loss. Focuses more on low-confidence examples by adding extra term in formula.

--Jaccard index (IoU)
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaccard_index
Very similar (if not same?) as dice loss

--Huber loss

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huber_loss

Used for "robust regression", less sensitive in outliers in data than squared error.
